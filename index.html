Questions

Straight answers.

The things everyone asks before they book.

Is it worth the money?+

If you pay around $300 a year to have lights hung, or you climb the ladder yourself every fall, the math breaks even in about 8 to 10 years. And you get year-round use, not six weeks. If you plan to move in a couple of years, it is probably not worth it.

What happens when a bulb dies?+

Single nodes can be swapped without redoing the whole run. Parts are covered by the 5-year product warranty. Labor for service calls is spelled out in your contract before we start.

How much does permanent Christmas lighting cost?+

Most homes land between $25 and $35 per foot installed, so a typical home runs a few thousand dollars total. Use the estimator above for a rough number, then get a free exact quote. No surprises.

How much electricity does it use?+

Very little. Each node runs about 0.6 watts, so a full roofline costs less per month than a porch light left on.

Will it bother my neighbors?+

No. The light washes your house, not theirs. Everything is dimmable and on timers. Most owners run a soft warm white nightly and save the color shows for holidays.

What if I sell my house?+

It stays with the house and transfers through the app. It photographs great for listings and it is a real curb-appeal talking point.

Can I just buy lights and do it myself?+

You can. A DIY kit runs around $700 in parts. What you do not get: a clean daytime look, matched peaks, warranty labor, or your weekends back. Professionally installed, even a DIY-brand system runs $1,500 to $4,000+.

Do they survive winter and ice?+

Yes. Sealed, outdoor-rated components built for freeze and thaw. We install March through November because the mounting sealant needs 40 degrees or warmer to cure.

How long does the install take? Do I need to be home?+

Most homes are done in one day. You do not need to be home. We just need exterior access and a place to plug in.

Do I control it from my phone?+

Yes. The app gives you 16 million colors, presets for every holiday, and timers. Set a warm white glow every night or go all out for game day.

What about my HOA?+

The track is color-matched to your trim and near-invisible during the day. We can provide product specs for your HOA review before you commit to anything. Budget 30 to 60 days for approval and start early.

What is the warranty?+

IronPeak backs the LEDs, controller, and power supply with a 5-year product warranty. If a part fails, it gets replaced.

Is this the same as permanent lighting?+

Yes. Permanent Christmas lights, permanent holiday lighting, and permanent roofline lighting are all names for the same thing: LEDs installed on your home year-round.
